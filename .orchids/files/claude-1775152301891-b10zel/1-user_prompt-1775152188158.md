Visual:

Kids speaking, presenting, laughing, interacting

Voice:

“So we redesigned the system.

Visual:

Clean diagram:
Cell = 5–6 students + 1 facilitator

Voice:

“Small learning cells.
High engagement. Real output.”

🎥 SCENE 5 — SPONSOR ENTRY (35–45 sec)

Visual:

Sponsor → funds cell → class activates

Voice:

“A sponsor activates one cell — fully visible, fully measurable.”

🎥 SCENE 6 — THE GUIDER SYSTEM 🔥 (45–65 sec) (THIS IS YOUR DIFFERENTIATOR)

Visual:

Student finishes → arrow down → becomes guider
New group forms under them
Lines connecting outcomes

Voice:

“Then the system compounds.

A student who completes the program moves forward —
and sends value down.

They become a guider for the next learning cell.

Now their results are tied to the students below them.

If those students grow — they grow.

If they don’t — it reflects back on them.

This creates accountability across levels.

Students are no longer just learners.
They are responsible for outcomes.”

🎥 SCENE 7 — ACCOUNTABILITY LOOP (65–75 sec)

Visual:

Circle loop animation:
Student → Guider → New Student → Results → Back up

Voice:

“Every layer influences the next.
And every result flows back up.”

🎥 SCENE 8 — COINS / VALUE SYSTEM (75–85 sec)

Visual:

Coins earned, shared, redistributed
Group score shown

Voice:

“Performance is shared.
Cells are graded together.
Value is distributed.
Effort becomes visible.”

🎥 SCENE 9 — COMPOUNDING (85–95 sec)

Visual:

One cell → multiple cells expanding

Voice:

“One cell becomes many.
Without losing structure.”

🎥 SCENE 10 — CLOSE (95–110 sec)

“This is not a donation.
