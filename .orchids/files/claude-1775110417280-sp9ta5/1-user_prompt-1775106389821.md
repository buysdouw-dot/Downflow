SHORT VIDEO STORY — “MY VOICE”
Aligned to English Learning & the Producing Levels
Duration: ~20–25 seconds
Tone: gentle, confident, human
Audience: children, parents, educators
Style: cinematic, soft light, minimal
STORY FLOW (ENGLISH + PRODUCING MODEL)
OPENING — SAFE SPACE (AWAKEN)
0–4 seconds
A quiet, open space.
Children stand together in soft shadow.
They are calm.
No pressure.
No expectation.
A warm light appears above, steady and kind.
CHILD 1 — AWAKEN (Awareness of Language)
The light touches the first child.
They step forward slightly.
Child says (simple English):
“My name is Max.
I am learning English.”
CHILD 2 — ALIGN (Understanding & Confidence)
The light flows downward, widening gently.
The second child rises.
Child says:
“My name is Lina.
I am not afraid to try.”
CHILD 3 — APPLY (Using English to Create)
The light becomes clearer and more structured.
The third child steps into it.
Child says:
“My name is Noah.
I use English to share my ideas.”
CHILD 4 — AMPLIFY (Speaking With Others)
The light spreads outward, connecting the group.
The fourth child looks toward the others.
Child says:
“My name is Aiko.
When I speak, others listen.”
CHILD 5 — ASCEND (Belonging & Fluency)
All children are now softly illuminated.
The fifth child steps forward — among the group.
Child says:
“My name is Maya.
English helps us connect.”
CLOSING — THE LOOP COMPLETES
Final 3–5 seconds
All children stand together in the light.
Relaxed. Equal. Confident.
Final line — spoken together, slowly and clearly:
“This is our voice.”
Fade out.
Leave space for title or logo after the scene.
WHY THIS VERSION IS POWERFUL
Uses very simple, correct English
Models confidence over complexity
Shows progression:
learning → trying → using → sharing → belonging
Parents immediately understand the value
Children can see themselves in the story
This silently communicates:
English is not about being perfect.
English is about being heard.
OPTIONAL MICRO-TWEAKS (ONLY IF NEEDED)
Swap final line to:
“English is our voice.”
Add soft subtitles for clarity
Use local children / mixed accents
